# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Endless Snake (Бесконечная Змейка)
**Developer:** Spilornis Team (Andrey Egorov, Elena Bazhenovy)
**Platform:** Android (minSdk 26)
**Effective date:** August 9, 2026
**Last updated:** August 9, 2026

---

## 1. General Provisions

This Privacy Policy describes how "Endless Snake" handles user information.

**Core principle:** The Application collects **only the minimum necessary** data. We **do not sell** personal data to third parties, except for the advertising partner explicitly listed below.

---

## 2. Current Version (v1.0.0)

The current version is **fully offline**, does not require internet, and stores data (high score, settings, save) **only locally** on your device. No personal data is collected or transmitted.

---

## 3. Future Versions (from v1.1.0) — VK Ads

Starting from version 1.1.0, the Application may display ads via the **VK Ads SDK** (LLC "V Kontakte", Russia).

### 3.1 Data collected by VK Ads SDK
- Advertising ID (GAID)
- Device model and OS version
- Anonymised IP address
- Ad impressions and clicks

VK Ads SDK **does not collect** name, phone, email, contacts, SMS, or other sensitive data.

### 3.2 Data processing
Data collected by VK Ads is processed according to [VK Privacy Policy](https://vk.com/privacy). The developer has no access to this data.

---

## 4. Your Rights

You can at any time:
- **Disable personalised ads:** Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out
- **Reset Advertising ID:** Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset
- **Withdraw consent:** Uninstall the Application (all local data is deleted automatically)

---

## 5. Children

The Application is rated **0+**. Ads comply with Russian Federal Law No. 38-FZ "On Advertising".

---

## 6. Security

All data is transmitted over **HTTPS/TLS 1.2+**. Local data is protected by Android OS isolation. The app is signed with RSA-2048 (APK Signature Scheme v2).
