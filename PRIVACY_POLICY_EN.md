1. General Provisions
This Privacy Policy describes how the Endless Snake application processes user information.
Core principle: The app collects the minimum necessary amount of data solely to ensure its functionality. We do not sell or disclose users' personal data to third parties, except for advertising partners explicitly mentioned in this Policy.

2. Current Version
2.1 Personal Data
The app does not collect personal data, including:

First name, last name, patronymic

Email address, phone number

Geolocation

Biometric data

Device identifiers (IMEI, MAC)

2.2 Local Data (device‑only)
The app stores exclusively locally (Android internal storage):

Game record — maximum score

Settings — language, sound volume, difficulty, control type

Game save — current game state

This data is never transmitted to external servers and is automatically removed when the app is uninstalled.

2.3 Analytics Module (myTracker)
The app uses the myTracker SDK (LLC «MyTracker») to collect anonymised usage statistics. This helps us improve the gaming experience and fix bugs.

Data processed by myTracker:

Advertising ID (GAID) – for audience measurement and update efficiency analysis

Device model and OS version – for adaptation to different devices

IP address (anonymised) – for region determination

In-app events (game completion, screen openings, skin unlocks, achievements and quests completion) – to understand user behaviour

Important: myTracker does not collect names, phone numbers, emails, contacts, SMS, message content, or any other sensitive data. All data is transmitted in anonymised form and used only in aggregated statistics.

Data processing is carried out in accordance with myTracker's Privacy Policy (https://tracker.my.com/ru/legal/privacy-policy). The App Developer does not have access to users' personal data through myTracker.

3. Advertising Module (VK Ads SDK)
The app displays advertisements via the VK Ads SDK (LLC «V Kontakte», INN 7707650905).

3.1 Why Internet Access Is Needed
To load ad banners and videos, the app requires the INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permissions. These permissions are used exclusively for ad serving — the game logic works offline.

3.2 Data Processed by VK Ads SDK
When displaying ads, the partner (VK) may automatically collect:

Data Type	Purpose
Advertising ID (GAID)	Showing relevant ads, limiting frequency
Device model and OS version	Adapting ad formats
IP address (anonymised)	Determining the display region
Ad impressions and clicks	Campaign statistics
Ad session identifier	Fraud protection
Important: VK Ads SDK does not collect names, phone numbers, emails, contacts, SMS, message content, or other sensitive data.

3.3 Transfer of Data to Third Parties
Data collected by the VK Ads SDK is processed in accordance with the VK Privacy Policy. The App Developer does not have access to personal data collected by the ad SDK and does not transfer it to other third parties.

4. User Rights
In accordance with Federal Law No. 152‑FZ «On Personal Data», you have the right:

4.1 Opt‑out of Personalised Advertising
Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of ad personalisation

4.2 Reset Advertising ID
Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Reset advertising ID

4.3 Withdraw Consent and Delete Data
Full withdrawal of consent is performed by uninstalling the App from your device. All local data is automatically deleted.

5. Children
The app has an age rating of 0+ and is suitable for users of any age:

No violence, profanity, or frightening content

No in‑app purchases

No social interaction between users

Ads are shown in accordance with the age restrictions of VK's advertising materials. Advertising age labelling complies with the requirements of Federal Law No. 38‑FZ «On Advertising».

6. Security
All data is transmitted over secure HTTPS/TLS 1.2+

Local data is protected by Android's storage isolation

The app is signed with an RSA‑2048 digital certificate (APK Signature Scheme v2)

VK Ads SDK undergoes independent security audits

7. Policy Changes
The Developer reserves the right to make changes to this Policy. The updated version will be published in this document with the date of revision.
